# Full description (4000)
Arrow Traffic is a calm arrow puzzle and brain game: tap colorful arrows off the board in the right order. One finger, fully offline, no ads. A perfect logic puzzle for a quick break, your commute or a few levels before bed.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow. If its path to the edge is clear, it slides off the board.
• If another arrow is in the way, it stays put. You don't lose, it just counts as a mistake.
• The queue at the top shows which color passes the gate next.
• The called color goes straight through; other colors wait in the pocket.
• When the queue reaches a color waiting in the pocket, it leaves in a chain.
• Fill the pocket and the level ends. Empty the board to win.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Learn the rules in a minute; finding the right order is a real logic puzzle.
• Every tap is a decision: send this arrow now, or let it wait?
• The small pocket rewards thinking ahead, and a well-planned chain feels great.
• No timers, no lives, no waiting. Play at your own pace.

FEATURES
• 1000 levels with a gentle difficulty curve from easy to tricky
• Every level is verified by a solver, so every puzzle has a solution
• Offline game: play on a plane, the subway, anywhere without Wi-Fi
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• One-finger portrait gameplay
• Easy-on-the-eyes dark theme plus a light theme
• Color-blind friendly: every color also carries its own symbol
• Progress saves automatically
• Small download, smooth even on older phones

WHO IT'S FOR
• Fans of brain games, logic games and puzzle games
• Anyone who wants a short mental workout during a break
• Players who enjoy sudoku, block puzzles or sorting games
• Anyone looking for a relaxing game that never rushes you
