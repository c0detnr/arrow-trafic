# App name
Arrow Traffic

# Short description
Clear arrows in the right order and keep your pocket from filling up.

# Full description
Arrow Traffic is a calm puzzle game where you clear colorful arrows off the board in the right order. It is played in portrait mode with one finger, and it works fully offline.

HOW TO PLAY
• The board holds colorful arrows pointing in different directions. Tap an arrow.
• If the path ahead is clear to the edge, the arrow slides off the board.
• If another arrow is in the way, it stays put. This does not make you lose; it only adds to your mistake count.
• The exit queue at the top shows which color is next.
• An arrow of the next color passes straight through the gate.
• An arrow of any other color drops into the waiting pocket.
• If the pocket is full, you lose the level.
• When the queue reaches a color already waiting in the pocket, those arrows leave in a chain.
• Clear every arrow from the board to win.

WHY IT IS FUN
• The rules take a minute to learn, but finding the right order takes real thought.
• Every move is a small decision: which arrow should leave now, and which should wait?
• The pocket is small, so planning ahead matters.
• A well-timed chain reaction feels very satisfying.

FEATURES
• 50 levels that ramp up gently from easy to tricky
• A daily challenge that refreshes every day
• A waiting pocket with 3 or 4 slots, depending on the level
• Six colors: red, blue, green, yellow, purple and orange
• One-finger controls in portrait orientation
• Offline play: on a commute, in a waiting room, anywhere without a connection
• Score and stars based on your mistake count

ADS
The game only offers optional rewarded ads. If you like, you can watch one to get a hint or to open an extra slot in the waiting pocket. You never have to watch an ad, and the game can be finished without them.

WHO IT IS FOR
• Anyone who wants a short mental workout during a break
• Fans of logic and sequencing puzzles
• Players who prefer a relaxed game that never rushes them

Clear the arrows in the right order, keep the pocket from overflowing and empty the board. Enjoy!
