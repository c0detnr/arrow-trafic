Arrow Traffic – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Arrow Traffic ("the app") is developed by c0detnr. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not request any Android permissions or use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your current level and theme choice are saved only in the app's private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
